Where is Thami Bennani?

Thami Bennani was a 17-year-old Moroccan teenager from Mohammedia who disappeared on 14 March 2007.

This independent public-interest archive documents the Thami Bennani case through a sourced timeline, published reporting, and attributed public statements.

Thami Bennani case timeline

The case returned to major public attention in 2022. A first-instance ruling in July 2023 sentenced two defendants to 20 years in prison each, and the family’s lawyers announced an intention to appeal.

The archive respects the presumption of innocence, attributes allegations and advocacy demands to their sources, and does not independently determine criminal responsibility.
